Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 13, 2026

Hadley Partners respects the privacy of people who visit this website or contact us. This policy explains the information we collect, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

Information we collect

We may collect information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, phone number, company name, and the business context you choose to share. We may also collect basic technical information about your browser, device, and use of the site.

How we use information

We use information to respond to inquiries, understand whether our advisory services may be relevant, communicate with you, improve the website, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

How we share information

We may share information with service providers that support hosting, email, analytics, and business operations, or with professional advisors when relevant to an engagement. We may also share information when required by law or with your consent. We do not sell personal information to unrelated third parties.

Confidential business information

Please do not send highly confidential or time-sensitive business information through this website unless appropriate confidentiality arrangements are in place. An initial inquiry does not create an advisory or confidentiality relationship.

Communications

If you contact us, we may respond by email or phone. You may opt out of non-essential communications by contacting us directly.

Security and retention

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards. No transmission or storage method is completely secure. We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for business, legal, or compliance purposes.

Your choices

Where legally available, you may ask to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you. You may also disable cookies through your browser settings.
